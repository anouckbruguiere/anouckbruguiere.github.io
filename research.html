Research

PhD project

AI-driven vibration analysis. Autonomous fault prediction for rotating machinery, in collaboration with LAUM (CNRS, UMR 6613) and OROS.

Supervisors: Kais Hassan, Jean-Hugh Thomas and Amaury Graillat.

In a few words

A rotating machine is never silent. Its vibrations carry information about how it is doing, and a fault often shows up in the signal long before it shows up anywhere else. My goal is to teach an algorithm to read these signals and to predict a developing fault on its own, without someone looking at every spectrum.

Machines I work on

  • Water turbines (splashy)
  • Fans (windy)
  • Ball mills (rocks in a blender)
