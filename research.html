Research

PhD project

Vibration analysis for rotating machines using deep learning. Autonomous fault prediction for rotating machinery, in collaboration with LAUM (CNRS, UMR 6613) and OROS.

Supervisors: Kais Hassan, Jean-Hugh Thomas and Amaury Graillat.

In a few words

Unplanned breakdowns of industrial rotating machinery can cost millions per incident. My PhD aims at an intelligent fault diagnosis framework that can detect and predict faults on its own, validated on a Francis turbine.

The work combines vibration analysis with deep learning for diagnosing mechanical defects such as ball-bearing faults, with a focus on models that experts can interpret. I also explore digital-twin approaches for predictive maintenance.

Machines I work on

  • Water turbines (splashy)
  • Fans (windy)
  • Ball mills (rocks in a blender)
